# Security policy

Please report suspected vulnerabilities through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting for this repository. If that channel is unavailable, contact
`d.o.mezhov@gmail.com` and do not open a public issue with exploit details.

Security-impacting fuzzing crashes are acknowledged within one business day and
target remediation within seven days. Other reproducible crashes are
acknowledged within three business days and target remediation within 30 days.
Published releases are supported until the next release is available.
